ShiftServe

A job-matching platform built exclusively for restaurant workers — connecting staff with full-time and part-time opportunities, faster.

Business overview

Problem we are solving
Workers struggle to find shifts that match their availability ▼
Most job portals show corporate roles. Restaurant staff rely on WhatsApp groups, word-of-mouth, or walking in with a resume. There is no dedicated fast-hiring tool for this segment.
Restaurants face constant last-minute vacancy crises ▼
A chef calling in sick or a busser quitting without notice can destroy a restaurant's service day. There is no fast, reliable platform to fill shifts within hours — not days.
Existing platforms are not built for this industry ▼
LinkedIn, Naukri, and Indeed are designed for white-collar hiring. They are too complex, require formal CVs, and do not understand shift-based employment, kitchen roles, or food service terminology.

Core app features
Organised by user type. Start with MVP features, expand in later phases.
For workers (job seekers)
  • Quick profile builder — role, experience, certifications, language, availability days/times
  • Shift-based search — filter by full-time / part-time / single shift, pay range, distance
  • One-tap apply — no long forms. Apply with profile + optional voice/video intro
  • Push notifications — alert for new nearby openings matching role
  • Work history & ratings — verified past experience, employer reviews visible to applicants
  • In-app chat — message employer after applying, confirm shift times
For restaurants (employers)
  • Fast job posting — role, shift, pay, location in under 2 minutes
  • Candidate shortlisting — swipe/filter interface to review applicants quickly
  • Verified profiles — Aadhaar-linked ID check, past employer confirmation
  • Interview scheduling — send slots, confirm bookings in-app
  • Worker ratings — rate hired staff; builds a reliability score over time
  • Talent pool / saved search — save filters, re-hire past workers instantly
Platform & admin features
  • Analytics dashboard — active listings, fill rate, avg time-to-hire, churn
  • Fraud detection — flag fake listings or fake worker profiles
  • Dispute resolution — worker-employer conflict flow
  • Billing & subscription — employer payment, invoicing, credits system

Risks & mitigation
Key challenges and how to navigate them proactively.
High
Low early liquidity (chicken-and-egg problem)
Workers won't join if there are no jobs; employers won't post if there are no workers. Mitigation: Manually onboard 50 restaurants and 500 workers in one city before launch. Offer first 3 months free to employers.
High
Competition from Apna, WorkIndia, or Zomato expanding into this space
Mitigation: Go deep, not wide — build features only restaurant workers need (shift scheduling, kitchen role taxonomy, FoH/BoH distinction). Niche moats.
Medium
Trust & fraud — fake profiles or ghost employers
Mitigation: Phone + Aadhaar verification for workers, GST verification for restaurants, rating system after each hire.
Medium
Low digital literacy among target workers
Mitigation: Vernacular language support (Tamil, Hindi, Telugu), WhatsApp-based onboarding fallback, voice-first UI option.
Low
Regulatory risk on gig worker classification
Mitigation: Position as a marketplace, not an employer. Monitor Code on Social Security amendments.
